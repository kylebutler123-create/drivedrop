# Customer app payments and receipts

The approved mobile Payments & receipts design applies below 1024px to the mobile website and installed home-screen app. The existing account subpage arrow links directly to `/account`, and Account remains selected in the bottom navigation. The shared header, logo and account avatar remain unchanged.

The navy summary shows the current filters' total paid, refunded and net paid amounts. The white statement card retains yearly selection, custom date validation, PDF statements and CSV export. Search, month and payment-status controls use the existing filtering helpers. Payment history uses compact cards with native expandable details, collection and delivery addresses, payment breakdown, paid date, receipt and booking links. Uploaded vehicle images have a car/van fallback, including images that fail before hydration. Unpaid records have no receipt action, and test records remain labelled. No sample amounts or images are included in application data.

Existing customer ownership checks, payment calculations, download endpoints and payment behaviour are unchanged. Desktop retains its table, selected-payment breakdown and existing controls. No database, Stripe or authentication changes are included.

Validation: TypeScript and production builds passed. The existing customer payments checks passed for totals, London dates, CSV safety, PDF generation, authentication, ownership and receipt eligibility. An isolated production-browser fixture passed layouts at 320, 390, 430, 768 and 1023px; long addresses, references and amounts; expansion/collapse; search/status/month filters; custom dates and errors; archived years; empty and unpaid states; test labels; vehicle images and failed-image fallback; receipt and statement request parameters; and the Account-to-payments round trip. The 1440px desktop comparison had no changes beyond a one-level RGB antialiasing tolerance. No browser runtime errors or write requests occurred. Fixture authentication and payments were synthetic, API responses were intercepted, and no real payment or account record was changed.
