# Mobile expanded delivery cards

Matches the approved expanded-card preview below 1024px. The existing mobile summary continues to show the vehicle, route, collection schedule, progress and contact actions. Expanded cards show four additional vehicle facts, one payment panel, transporter next actions, and collapsible evidence. Customer cards also retain their delivery history disclosure.

`MobileDeliveryDetails.tsx` contains presentation components. Amounts, payment status, payout status and refund adjustments come from the existing role-scoped booking response. No pricing, payment or eligibility rules change. Signed collection/delivery reports use the same authorised evidence URLs and retain timestamps, condition notes, signatures and location information. The existing upload, proof, confirmation, dispute, cancellation, review and receipt controls remain mounted with their original handlers.

The older identity/route/payment nodes remain available to the desktop layout and selector-based enhancers, but are hidden on mobile where their information is already shown. The transporter app hides the expanded Delivery history panel at the user's request; the compact progress indicator remains visible, and historical records are preserved for desktop. The sibling action panel and collection/delivery evidence stay available. Customer history keeps dated updates and notes. Messages remain accessible through the summary's existing conversation link. All new styles are scoped to the mobile breakpoint and customer/transporter roles.

The transporter mobile summary uses the approved navy details button (`#071c43`) with white text and arrow, blue Message circle (`#2563eb`) with a white glyph, and green Call circle (`#16a34a`) with a white glyph. Collection and delivery action buttons retain their orange style and use the same 16px font size and bold (700) weight as the details button in both compact and expanded cards. The same colours remain when the details are expanded. These overrides do not affect customer cards, job/quote buttons or desktop.

The real-map design reference uses a sample Manchester–Birmingham route. It is not wired into booking cards: jobs currently store address strings, not collection/delivery coordinates or route geometry. A geocoding and routing provider needs to be connected before the real map can represent each booking. Do not embed the sample map as actual delivery data. The existing proof-of-delivery location map remains separate and unchanged.

Validation uses isolated customer/transporter fixtures and intercepted API requests: 320/390/430/768px layouts, disclosure and collapse behaviour, dispute drafting/cancellation, collection and delivery forms, status drafting, cancellation prompt, delivered confirmation, signed evidence, refunds, completed receipts/reviews, cancelled bookings, active disputes and missing payment data. No live payment, booking, message or evidence mutations are performed. TypeScript and the production build are checked before preview publication.
